🎀 Style Guide

Choose a styling tip and learn how to improve your outfit.

🎨 Color Matching

Pair soft colors with neutral shades. Use two or three coordinating colors to keep your outfit balanced.

💎 Accessory Styling

Use necklaces, earrings, bracelets, or hair accessories to give a simple outfit more personality.

🧥 Layering

Add a cardigan, blazer, jacket, or light outer layer to create depth and make your outfit more interesting.

👠 Shoe Matching

Match your shoes with the occasion. Flats work for casual looks while heels can make an outfit more elegant.

👜 Bag Matching

Choose a bag that complements your outfit without making the overall look feel too crowded.

🤍 Minimalist Styling

Keep your look clean by combining basic pieces with simple accessories and neutral colors.
